# Appealing a rejection

Policy: POL-06 (appeals), POL-04 (lifecycle), POL-08 (identity).

1. **Verify identity** with `get_permit`. Read `status`, `reason` and `appeal_deadline`.
2. Only `rejected` applications can be appealed. The deadline is **30 calendar days** after the decision date.
   Give the exact `appeal_deadline` date. If it has passed, say so; a new application is the only route.
3. Explain what the appeal needs: the grounds, and any new documents that answer the rejection reason.
4. The Appeals Committee decides within **15 working days**; its decision is final at the municipal level.
5. Rafeeq **cannot** file the appeal or predict its outcome. The applicant files it on the portal.
6. Alternatives to mention once: a new, corrected application, or a refund (fee − 100 SAR, see the
   `refund-request` skill).
