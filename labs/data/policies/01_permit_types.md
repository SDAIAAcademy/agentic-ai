# Permit types and required documents

Madar Municipal Services issues five permit types. Every application is submitted on the Madar portal by the
**applicant** (the owner) or an **authorized agent** holding a valid power of attorney.

| Code | Permit | Required documents |
|---|---|---|
| BLD | Building permit | title deed; engineering drawings stamped by a licensed engineering office; site plan; contractor contract |
| REN | Renovation permit | title deed; description of works; photos of the current state; for structural works, a structural engineer's report |
| SGN | Commercial signage permit | commercial registration (CR); shop lease or title deed; sign design with dimensions; for illuminated signs, an electrical safety certificate |
| FTR | Food truck permit | vehicle registration; a valid health certificate for every worker; a passed municipal health inspection |
| EVT | Temporary event permit | site plan; safety plan; Civil Defense approval when more than 500 attendees are expected |

## Rules that apply to every type

- An application missing any required document is moved to **needs_info**. See POL-04 for the deadline.
- Permits are issued to a person or a company, not to a property. They cannot be transferred.
- Starting work, operating or holding an event **without a valid permit** is a violation (see POL-07).
- Only a licensing officer can approve or reject an application. Digital assistants may explain the rules,
  check status for a verified applicant, compute fees and book inspections, but never decide an application.

## Type-specific rules

- **BLD**: two inspections are mandatory, at foundation and at completion.
- **REN**: works are *structural* when they touch columns, beams, slabs or load-bearing walls.
- **SGN**: a sign face larger than **12 m²** needs a review by the Urban Design Committee, which adds 10 working days.
- **FTR**: trucks may operate only in designated food-truck zones (POL-09), from 16:00 to 01:00.
- **EVT**: apply at least **20 calendar days** before the first event day. An event permit covers at most **14 days**.

## الملخص بالعربية

تصدر خدمات مدار البلدية خمسة أنواع من التصاريح: رخصة بناء (BLD)، ورخصة ترميم (REN)، ورخصة لوحة تجارية (SGN)،
وتصريح عربة طعام (FTR)، وتصريح فعالية مؤقتة (EVT). يقدّم الطلب المالك أو وكيل بوكالة سارية.
الطلب الناقص ينتقل إلى حالة «بحاجة إلى معلومات». الموظف المرخِّص وحده يوافق على الطلب أو يرفضه، والمساعد الرقمي
لا يتخذ القرار. اللوحة التي تتجاوز مساحتها 12 م² تحتاج إلى مراجعة لجنة التصميم العمراني (10 أيام عمل إضافية).
عربات الطعام تعمل في المناطق المخصصة فقط من 4 مساءً إلى 1 صباحًا. الفعالية يُقدَّم طلبها قبل 20 يومًا على الأقل،
ومدتها 14 يومًا كحد أقصى.
