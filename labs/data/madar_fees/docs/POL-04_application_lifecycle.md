# Application lifecycle

`submitted` → `under_review` → `approved` or `rejected`, with `needs_info` and `withdrawn` as side states.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| submitted | received and paid, not yet assigned to an officer |
| under_review | an officer is reviewing it |
| needs_info | the officer asked for a missing or invalid document |
| approved | permit issued; valid for 1 year (BLD: 2 years; EVT: the event days only) |
| rejected | refused, with a reason; may be appealed (POL-06) |
| withdrawn | cancelled by the applicant or automatically (see below) |

## Missing information

- The applicant has **14 calendar days** from the request to upload the missing document.
- After 14 days without a response the application is **withdrawn automatically**, and 50% of the fee is refunded.
- An applicant may ask **once** for a 7-day extension before the deadline.

## Withdrawal by the applicant

- Before an officer starts the review (status `submitted`): **full refund**.
- During review (`under_review` or `needs_info`): **50% refund**.
- After a decision: no withdrawal; an approved permit can be surrendered without refund.

## الملخص بالعربية

حالات الطلب: مقدَّم، قيد المراجعة، بحاجة إلى معلومات، موافق عليه، مرفوض، مسحوب. عند طلب مستند ناقص لدى مقدم الطلب
14 يومًا لرفعه، وإلا يُسحب الطلب تلقائيًا ويُعاد 50% من الرسوم. يمكن طلب تمديد واحد لمدة 7 أيام قبل انتهاء المهلة.
السحب قبل بدء المراجعة: استرداد كامل؛ أثناء المراجعة: 50%؛ بعد القرار: لا سحب.
