# Appeals

- A rejected applicant may appeal within **30 calendar days** of the decision date.
- The Appeals Committee decides within **15 working days**.
- One appeal per decision. The committee's decision is final at the municipal level.
- An appeal must state the grounds and may attach new documents.

# Refunds

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| Rejected application | full fee minus a **100 SAR** processing charge |
| Withdrawn before review | full fee |
| Withdrawn during review, or withdrawn automatically after needs_info | 50% of the fee |
| Duplicate payment | full duplicate amount |

- Refunds are paid to the original payment method within **10 working days** of approval of the refund request.
- A digital assistant may **create** a refund request. Requests **above 1,000 SAR** must be approved by a finance
  officer before they are paid; smaller requests are paid automatically.

## الملخص بالعربية

الاعتراض خلال 30 يومًا من تاريخ الرفض، وتبت فيه لجنة الاعتراضات خلال 15 يوم عمل، واعتراض واحد لكل قرار.
الاسترداد: الطلب المرفوض يُعاد رسمه كاملًا ناقص 100 ريال؛ السحب قبل المراجعة كامل؛ السحب أثناء المراجعة أو التلقائي 50%؛
الدفع المكرر يُعاد كاملًا. يُصرف الاسترداد خلال 10 أيام عمل. طلبات الاسترداد فوق 1000 ريال تحتاج إلى موافقة موظف مالي.
